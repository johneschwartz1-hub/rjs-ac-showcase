# 15-Minute Demo Script: Service Intake to Quote

**Scenario:** a fictional request from Avery Example, whose A/C runs but does not cool.
**Principle:** open with the customer's problem from discovery, show only what answers it, end with a decision.

## Setup (before the call)
- Demo environment loaded with the fictional record only; no real customer data.
- Browser tabs open: Service Queue, Calculator, print preview.
- Know the numbers: 2 hours labor at $100, $80 materials with 25% markup, 7% example tax, **$321** total.
- Backup: screenshots of each step in case of connectivity trouble.

## 1. Frame the problem (1 min)
"On discovery you told us requests arrive incomplete, and your team spends time chasing details before they can quote. I'll show how a request goes from intake to a reviewed estimate."

## 2. Intake (3 min)
- Enter the typed request. 
- Point out the "Information to confirm" panel: requested service, urgency, preferred window.
- **Say:** "The system flags what's missing instead of guessing, so nothing incomplete moves forward."

## 3. Review and approval (3 min)
- Open the request in the Service Queue; fill in the missing fields.
- Try approving while incomplete: it is blocked.
- Approve explicitly.
- **Say:** "Your operator stays in control. Nothing becomes a quote until a person approves it."

## 4. Quote (5 min)
- Open the linked draft.
- Add labor and materials; show the markup and the $321 total.
- Save and open print preview.
- **Say:** "Pricing is a separate decision from intake, so the person who knows costs sets the number."

## 5. Close (3 min)
- Recap the three outcomes: fewer incomplete requests, operator control, consistent estimates.
- Be upfront: records are browser-local today; shared storage, sign-in, and cloud deployment are planned.
- Ask: "What would you need to see to run a pilot? Who else should see this?"
- Agree next step and date.

## Likely questions
| Question | Answer approach |
|---|---|
| Can it connect to our system? | Ask what and who owns it; scope in SOW rather than promise. |
| Where is data stored? | Be accurate about current state; describe the plan separately. |
| How many users? | Share what has been tested; offer a pilot to validate. |
| What does it cost? | Hand to sales; note what drives scope (users, integrations, hosting). |

## After
Send recap, screenshots, open questions, and the proposed pilot scope within 24 hours.
