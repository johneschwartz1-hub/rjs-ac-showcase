# Discovery Call Template

**Goal:** leave with enough detail to tailor the demo, scope the solution, and judge whether we can win. 30–45 minutes, with the sales lead on the call.

## Before the call
- Review the account: size, locations, systems already in use, any prior contact.
- Agree roles with sales: who runs the call, who takes notes, who owns follow-up.
- Send a short agenda and ask who else should attend (operations, IT, finance).

## Opening (3 min)
Confirm time, attendees, and the outcome we want: "By the end, we should both know whether a demo is worth your time and what it should cover."

## 1. Current workflow (10 min)
- Walk me through what happens from the moment a customer request arrives to the moment it is resolved.
- Who touches it? What tools or paper are involved?
- Where do requests get lost, delayed, or re-entered?

## 2. Impact (5 min)
- How many requests or transactions per day, per location, at peak?
- What does a missed or incomplete request cost: time, rework, customer satisfaction?
- What is driving this now? Any deadline, event, or budget cycle?

## 3. Requirements and priorities (8 min)
- Must-haves vs nice-to-haves.
- Different user types or service levels (for example priority customers, accessibility needs).
- Reporting: who needs which numbers, and how often?

## 4. Technical and security (7 min)
- Systems we would need to connect to (CRM, payment, scheduling, third-party apps). Who owns each?
- Hosting preference: cloud, on-premises, no preference.
- Security or compliance requirements, and who reviews vendors.
- Devices, network limits, and locations.

## 5. Decision process (5 min)
- Who decides, who influences, who can block?
- Timeline, budget range, and how they will compare options.
- Success criteria for a pilot.

## Close (2 min)
Summarize what I heard, confirm the demo scope, agree on next steps and dates.

## After the call (within 24 hours)
- Send a recap and the agreed demo scope.
- Log in the deal notes: pain, impact, requirements, integrations, security questions, decision process, risks.
- Flag open questions for the SOW.
